## 2.1 The Arithmetic Substrate (IST)

### Non-Technical: The Digital Photograph

Look at a photograph on a screen. It looks smooth. The gradient of a sunset, the curve of a face, the texture of fabric — all continuous. But zoom in far enough, and the image dissolves into pixels. Each pixel has a specific color value. The apparent continuity is an illusion produced by the density of the pixels.

The theory begins with a version of this observation: what if reality itself is like this? What if the smooth continuum of space, time, and quantum fields is an appearance, produced by a substrate that is fundamentally discrete?

If that were true, two things would follow immediately. First, the discreteness would have to be extremely fine, or we would have noticed it already. Second, the discrete units would not be arranged on a simple grid. They would be arranged in a structure with its own geometry — a structure that determines which configurations are possible and which are not.

This is the central hypothesis of Invariant Set Theory: **the universe is a deterministic dynamical system on a discrete, finite-information state space, and the physically realizable configurations form a proper subset of all conceivable configurations.**

The subset is the invariant set. It is like the set of legal positions in chess. On a chessboard, there are more possible arrangements of pieces than anyone could count. But only a tiny fraction of those arrangements can be reached through legal play from the starting position. The legal positions form a structured set within the larger space of all arrangements.

The invariant set `I_U` plays the same role. It is the set of states reachable through the substrate's dynamics. Most conceivable states are not on it. The states on it are the physically real ones.

### Non-Technical: Postal Codes and the p-adic Metric

The invariant set does not have an everyday geometry. Its "distance" is not about meters or inches. It is hierarchical, like a postal code.

Consider two addresses: 10001 and 10002. They are in the same city, one postal-code digit apart. Now consider 10001 and 90001. They are in different cities, thousands of miles apart. The postal code tells you how "close" two addresses are by the depth of their shared structure, not by physical distance.

The p-adic metric works exactly like this. Two numbers are "close" if they differ by a high power of a prime `p`. This is a hierarchical distance: it measures shared structure, not spatial separation. The p-adic numbers form a tree-like structure in which neighborhoods are nested rather than arranged side by side.

The substrate is p-adic. This means its state space is not a grid; it is a hierarchy. The dynamics move states through this hierarchy. The invariant set is a fractal subset of it.

### Non-Technical: Why Discreteness is Invisible

The final non-technical point about IST is the finite information capacity `L`. In the digital photograph analogy, `L` is the resolution. If the resolution is high enough, the pixels are invisible. The theory requires `L` to be enormous — roughly 10^100 — so that the discreteness is far below any scale we can probe.

This is not a prediction. It is a hypothesis. The theory says: if the substrate is discrete, its granularity must be extremely fine. If experiments someday detect deviations from quantum mechanics at some scale, that scale would be the substrate's resolution.

### Technical: The Formalism

**State space.** Let `X` be a discrete, finite-information set equipped with a p-adic (ultrametric) structure. A point `x ∈ X` is a substrate configuration.

**Dynamics.** Let `F: X → X` be a deterministic map. The dynamics is the iteration `x_{n+1} = F(x_n)`.

**p-adic structure.** The p-adic metric is defined by `|x - y|_p = p^{-v_p(x-y)}`, where `v_p` is the p-adic valuation. The metric is ultrametric: `|x - z|_p ≤ max(|x - y|_p, |y - z|_p)`.

**Invariant set.** The invariant set `I_U ⊂ X` is the set of states that are physically realizable. It is characterized by an arithmetic admissibility condition: the canonical height `ĥ_F(x)` vanishes.

**Canonical height.** For an algebraic self-map `F` of degree `d ≥ 2`, the canonical height `ĥ_F: X → ℝ≥0` satisfies `ĥ_F(F(x)) = d · ĥ_F(x)`. The preperiodic locus is `{x : ĥ_F(x) = 0}`.

**Finite information capacity.** The substrate has finite capacity `L`, meaning that the number of distinguishable states in any region is bounded. `L` is not hand-set; it is derived as a monotone information quantity along the RG trajectory.

**Fractal fine structure.** The invariant measure `μ_*` on `I_U` has fine structure below the crossover scale `ξ₀`. Above `ξ₀`, the measure appears absolutely continuous. Below, it has arithmetic texture.

**Measurement-independence violation.** The substrate's structure correlates measurement settings and hidden variables through the shared p-adic geometry. This is not a conspiracy; it is a consequence of the substrate's own structure.

Asymptotic Safety says that gravity has a fixed point at high energies. This means that quantum gravity is well-defined at all scales: no infinities, no breakdown, just a smooth approach to a scale-invariant description.

### Non-Technical: How AS Binds IST to BM

Without AS, IST and BM would be juxtaposed. With AS, they are connected: IST is the UV, BM is the IR, and AS is the flow between them.

### Technical: The Formalism

**Effective action.** Let `Γ_k` be the effective action at scale `k`. It is the description of physics at scale `k`, including all quantum fluctuations above that scale.

**RG flow.** The flow equation is

```
∂_s Γ_s = (1/2) Tr[(Γ_s^(2) + R_s)^(-1) ∂_s R_s]
```

where `s = ln k`, `Γ_s^(2)` is the second functional derivative of the effective action, and `R_s` is the regulator.

**Fixed point.** A fixed point `Γ_*` satisfies `β[Γ_*] = 0`, where `β = ∂_s Γ_s`.

**Critical surface.** The critical surface `W^s(Γ_*)` is the set of all `Γ` that flow to `Γ_*` as `s → -∞`.

**Relevant directions.** The linearized flow around `Γ_*` gives eigenvalues `θ_i`. Positive `θ_i` correspond to relevant directions (parameters that matter in the IR); negative `θ_i` correspond to irrelevant directions (parameters that wash out).

**Universality.** Different microscopic theories flow to the same fixed point if they lie on the same critical surface. The IR behavior depends only on the relevant directions.

**Scheme independence.** Predictions must be stable under changes of regulator and truncation. A quantity that moves under legitimate scheme changes is a coordinate artifact, not a physical prediction.

**Spectral dimension.** The effective dimension at scale `k` is

```
d_s(k) = -2 d ln P(k) / d ln k
```

where `P(k)` is the return probability of a random walk at scale `k`. The theory predicts `d_s → 4` in the IR and `d_s → 2` in the UV.

**The bridge to IST.** The fixed point `Γ_*` in theory space corresponds to the invariant set `I_U` in state space. The correspondence is established by a map `Φ: X → T` (from substrate to theory space) and the relation

```
I_U = Φ^(-1)(W^s(Γ_*)).
```

This is a theorem target, not an assumption.

## 5. Typed Mathematical Spaces

The most important discipline of v6.3 is to keep mathematical objects in the spaces where they actually live.

Let:

- `X` = microscopic state space;
- `M(X)` = probability measures on `X`;
- `T` = effective-action / theory space;
- `O` = observable space.

An RG transformation acts on theory descriptions:

```
R_s : T → T
```

A microscopic update acts on states:

```
F : X → X
```

An observable construction acts toward measurable quantities:

```
Π : X → O
```

or, for measures:

```
Π_# : M(X) → M(O)
```

These are not interchangeable.

### 5.1 Fixed Point versus Invariant Set

Let `Γ_* ∈ T` be a candidate fixed point, `R_s(Γ_*) = Γ_*`. Let `W^s(Γ_*) ⊂ T` be its stable/critical surface.

Instead construct a correspondence `Φ : X → T` and test the theorem target:

```
I_crit =? Φ^(-1)(W^s(Γ_*))

Only after such a map exists can the old symbol `I_U` be justified as a physical invariant set.

with an explicit tolerance or an exact theorem.

## 9. The Amplitude Problem Is a First-Class Gate

The difficult problem is not merely deriving Fisher information or writing down the Madelung equations. It is constructing a complex amplitude sector that is genuinely generated by the microscopic dynamics and closed under evolution.

### 9.1 What Is Already Known

### 9.2 Native p-adic Character Route

This is not promoted as the answer. It is a high-priority workbench hypothesis. Required tests:

### 9.3 Chaos-Tameness / Tame-Factor Ladder

For a genuinely mixing measure-preserving system, the Koopman operator has no non-constant eigenfunctions. Therefore the program should not search for the quantum phase as an ordinary point-spectrum sector of the same mixing dynamics that explains statistical relaxation.

```

where `G` is zero-entropy, equicontinuous, or odometer-type and can support a nontrivial unit-circle character group. In the prime-power case, the natural candidate is the `p^∞` root-of-unity spectrum of the adding-machine class, which connects directly to the p-adic additive-character workbench.

**T1:** invariant measure and transfer/Koopman objects exist.

**T3:** the factor supports the required `U(1)` phase structure, potentially through `χ_p`.

The cheap Phase-2 diagnostic is a Kronecker/point-spectrum computation on the smallest admissible approximants, followed by a projective/infinite-limit consistency test. If the limiting system is weakly mixing and no tame factor exists, the amplitude route is killed for that substrate.

## 11. Born Probability: Exact Where It Means Exact

### 11.1 B0 — Equilibrium Measure

### 11.2 B1 — Regularity

### 11.3 B2 — Born Identification at the IR Observable Algebra

The exact target is:

```

```

where `Π_IR` is explicitly defined as the IR observable/projection map.

At finite resolution, the program allows a correction term:

```

```

with the requirement that `δ_{ξ,ε} → 0` in the declared Born regime and that any residual structure remain below the observational resolution or satisfy a separately declared bound.

Thus "Born exact" and "arithmetic texture" are not contradictory statements because they are statements about different projections/scales.

### 11.4 No-Signaling Constraint

For settings `a,b`, write the finite-resolution residual as `δρ_{a,b}(x_A,x_B)`. No-signaling requires the marginals to be independent of the remote setting:

```
Δ_A(x_A; a,b) = ∫ δρ_{a,b}(x_A,x_B) dx_B
Δ_A(x_A; a,b) = Δ_A(x_A; a)
Δ_B(x_B; a,b) = Δ_B(x_B; b)
```

for all allowed settings. Any nontrivial residual may therefore live only in joint correlators, not in controllable marginal changes.

If a correction permits controllable signaling for any settings family, the theory fails regardless of its other virtues.

### 11.5 B3 — Relaxation

Valentini's coarse-grained H-theorem supplies a relaxation mechanism under its hypotheses:

```
H[P|ρ] = ∫ P ln(P/ρ) dx
```

The program requires an appropriate coarse-graining and a demonstrable dynamical mixing regime. The key methodological distinction:

```
Born destination ≠ relaxation path
```

### 11.6 Typicality

Standard BM typicality is not a fourth independent theory of probability. It is treated as an interpretive/measure-theoretic benchmark: if the derived equilibrium measure is the Born measure, does the resulting typicality structure coincide with the usual BM statement?

## 14. The Deeper Pattern

The tripartite program's central question is: **can a discrete substrate produce quantum mechanics without assuming it?**

Every QG theory listed above has independently arrived at some version of this question:

- LQG: **yes**, for geometry (discrete area, volume).
- CDT: **yes**, for spacetime (emergent de Sitter).
- Causal sets: **yes**, for Lorentz invariance (order + counting).
- Wolfram: **yes**, for both GR and QM (hypergraph rewriting).
- 't Hooft: **yes**, for QM (cellular automaton).

The tripartite program asks the same question with a **different substrate**: arithmetic, not geometric, not computational. The convergence of so many independent approaches on the same answer — **yes, discreteness can produce the observed continuum** — is the strongest available evidence that the tripartite program's central hypothesis is not implausible.

The tripartite program does not need to adopt any of these theories. It needs to **borrow their proofs of concept** and apply them to an arithmetic substrate. The net-valid insights above are the ones that close specific gates or supply concrete constructions the program currently lacks.
