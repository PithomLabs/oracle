One framing sentence: the roadmap is organized as nine priority tiers plus three standing layers, ordered by information-gain-per-cost with every tier ending in a binary branch decision — attack, escalate within the frozen budget, or kill — and each tier names its gates, its borrowed ladders, and its kill condition, so that at any moment the program's status is readable as "current tier, current branch."

**Group A — TIER 0: The Freeze (weeks; everything downstream is void without it)**

1 - **Freeze the substrate-class budget** (§1.3.1, pre-registered, no widening): class 1 = globally algebraic degree-2 map, explicit $p=2$ local realization; class 2 = $p=3$ analogue; class 3 = multi-place $p=2\times3$; class 4 = non-arithmetic symbolic/graph control. Any addition requires a written, pre-registered reason. *Closes the search-class absorption channel.*
2 - **Freeze the null-model roster** (now instantiable from the survey): plain-AS/effective-flow control; BM+Valentini without arithmetic; standard BM typicality; Wolfram-class deterministic non-arithmetic substrate; GFT-condensate collective-mode account; CDT/causal-set stochastic discrete account. *Rule: every later prediction must differ from ≥1 of these or it is not evidence.*
3 - **Pre-register the theory-level claim families**: the Valentini fork (is sustained laboratory non-equilibrium preparable?); the no-signaling residual family indexed by settings; the T2/T3 shape families (colored-noise spectrum class, log-periodic oscillation class, Diophantine angular class) — frozen before any derivation touches data. *Contamination discipline made operational.*
4 - **Instantiate governance**: independent reviewer assigned per claim (different model family / named human, never the constructing instance); external red-team scheduled at every tier boundary; versioned review artifacts; unpromotion protocol with written reasons. *Closes the self-grading hole.*

**Group B — TIER 1: The Frozen Substrate (Phase 1; G1, G2)**

5 - **Compatibility library before bespoke physics**: run the smallest well-understood degree-2 algebraic maps; test whether canonical-height applicability and Markov/symbolic structure *coexist on one map class at all*. If the library contains no joint example, G2 becomes a published obstruction — a result, not a delay. *Cheapest possible kill; borrowed ladder: arithmetic-dynamics literature, not invention.*
6 - **Build and freeze $F_L$** (Deliverable A): declare the full tuple $(X_L, L, \text{encoding}, F_L, \text{placewise action}, \text{conventions}, \text{parameters})$; invoke the **A-freeze rule** — downstream results may never improve by modifying the substrate; a change creates a new candidate and restarts dependent tests.
7 - **Deliverables B–F on the same frozen object, in parallel**: B — height applicability with exact hypotheses; C — Markov applicability on the *same map* (no convenience swap); D — transfer/Koopman diagnostics; E — prime scan + $\chi_p$ closure test; F — the non-arithmetic null substrate of comparable complexity.
8 - **Branch decision T1**: no faithful $F_L$ → arithmetic branch stops; $F_L$ exists but fails joint requirements → escalate to class 2 within budget, never silently replace.

**Group C — TIER 2: The Amplitude Gate (G3 — the program's unique novel gate; highest scientific priority)**

9 - **Kronecker/point-spectrum computation on the smallest admissible approximants** — cheap, decisive, pre-registrable: finite systems always have discrete spectra; the test is whether a nontrivial factor survives coherently as $L \to \infty$.
10 - **Tame-factor search**: test for an odometer-type ($p^\infty$ root-of-unity) factor of the frozen dynamics — the one structural route by which a mixing system carries phase; if the limit is weakly mixing with no factor and no defensible alternative, the amplitude route dies *for that substrate*.
11 - **$\chi_p$ closure battery**: defined on required state classes; respects composition; closes under $F_L$; supports a nontrivial amplitude with physical norm; phase survives coarse-graining. *Borrowed ladders: adelic spectral triples (structure); twistor/positive-geometry (target shape for what the amplitude sector may be).*
12 - **Deliverable: the T1–T4 amplitude-factor report** — the required artifact; no Phase 3 work begins without it.
13 - **Branch decision T2**: no tame factor → escalate class once; second failure → the arithmetic-phase hypothesis is dead and the program pivots or terminates honestly at its central gate.

**Group D — TIER 3: The Measure (G4)**

14 - **Branch-specific theorem shelf, strictly enforced**: deterministic pre-trace — transfer/Perron–Frobenius operators, Lasota–Yorke estimates, ergodic decomposition, Kronecker-factor theory; post-trace stochastic — RDS, strong-Feller methods. *RDS theorems may never be cited for the deterministic map.*
15 - **Deliverables**: unique invariant measure $\mu_*$; mixing diagnostics; absolute-continuity and Fisher-regularity audit at observable scales; initial-condition analysis (`needInitialCondition` first filing).
16 - **Cross-validation**: compute the fine-structure constant $c$ two ways — Lyapunov route now, critical-exponent route at Tier 6; agreement is universality evidence, disagreement is an inconsistency gate.

**Group E — TIER 4: The Bridges (G5; the program's defining obligation)**

17 - **One-qubit projection prototype** $\pi_L: X_L \to \mathbb{C}^2$ answering the seven mandatory questions (domain, codomain, preserved, discarded, induced topology/measure, phase representation, post-projection dynamics). *The heart of the program.*
18 - **$\Phi$ prototype**: $\Phi_s = \mathcal{A}_s \circ \mathcal{C}_s$ with $\mathcal{A}_s$ reconstructing effective-action classes from a pre-registered finite correlator family; test the commutation condition $\Phi_{s'}(\mathcal{C}_{s\to s'}x) \simeq \mathfrak{R}_{s\to s'}(\Phi_s(x))$ with explicit tolerance. *Borrowed ladders: CFS projection techniques; HKLL-style reconstruction; CDT diffusion probes for induced geometry.*
19 - **Entanglement-geometry probe** (from the holographic dictionary): test whether $\mu_*$'s correlation structure yields $\mathcal{C}_{\rm eff}$'s metric — Gate C and P0 as one computation if it works.

**Group F — TIER 5: The Flow (dFRG; Phase 4)**

20 - **Build $\mathcal{C}_s$ on the frozen substrate**, then answer the six dFRG questions (mode, regulator, theory-space object, composition law, trace meaning, universal-vs-coordinate). *First borrowed ladder: tensorial group field theory RG — the only existing RG-on-combinatorial-DOF construction; adapt, do not reinvent.*
21 - **Fixed-point campaign**: existence, stability matrix, relevant directions; parameter count must match $N_{\rm rel}$ or the excess is a structural warning.
22 - **Scheme-independence study**: multiple regulators × multiple truncations × convergence; any derived constant that moves is an artifact, not a prediction.
23 - **Spectral dimension** $d_s(k)$ via CDT-methodology transfer; compare against the five-way convergence datum; flat-4 is a real possible outcome, not a failure to be repaired.
24 - **Branch decision T3**: no regulator-stable fixed point or non-converging truncations → AS-specific machinery demoted per D21; the program continues on effective-flow discipline alone or re-scopes.

**Group G — TIER 6: The IR Derivation (G6–G8; Phase 5, order non-negotiable)**

25 - **Strict sequence**: configuration space → amplitude → phase → generator → continuity → guidance → quantum potential → Born → asymptotic unitarity. A correct density without the generator is not quantum mechanics.
26 - **Internal-environment gate first**: exhibit the tensor factorization or conditional-expectation structure licensing the trace — without it the bath is a metaphor.
27 - **The trace computation** (the long campaign's center): output memory kernel $K(t-s)$, colored noise $\eta(t)$, fluctuation-dissipation check; Langevin before Kramers; Kramers only under controlled Markovian limit. *Borrowed ladder: Einstein–Langevin/noise-kernel machinery for the geometric side.*
28 - **Born identification** at the IR observable algebra with the residual $\delta_{\xi,\epsilon} \to 0$ in the declared regime; **no-signaling audit** on marginals across the settings family. *Any controllable marginal dependence = immediate death.*

**Group H — TIER 7: Composition and Bell (G9)**

29 - **Two weakly coupled subsystems**: tensor-product emergence; entanglement generation; Bell statistics.
30 - **Double-counting audit**: does the mechanism require both nonlocal guidance *and* setting-dependence for the same correlations? Redundancy is simplified away, not rationalized.
31 - **Foliation behavior test**: covariance/no-signaling in the declared regime; the locality-swap constructibility test (twistor-lesson) if effective nonlocality appears — representation map or the artifact claim stays refused.

**Group I — TIER 8: The Recovery Ladder (G10)**

32 - **E→L continuation first** (blocking gate for real-time trajectories).
33 - **Lorentz recovery with the Hořava failure map as checklist**: the extra scalar mode and surviving Lorentz-violating operators are the named traps; QFT-R1 passes only past them.
34 - **No-go audit** (Haag, Coleman–Mandula, Weinberg–Witten): assumptions listed, held/failing identified, IR re-emergent structure shown — "UV cutoff" alone is not an explanation.
35 - **Gravity as equation of state**: target the Jacobson method — Clausius-type relation on traces → GR-R0–R2 as thermodynamic consequence; entanglement-equilibrium route couples this to the Tier 4 probe. *If the metric is downstream, one computation serves Gate C and the recovery ladder.*
36 - **AS-matter compatibility**; **$\xi_0 = \ell_P$ cross-check** with LQG horizon-counting as the independent rival method.

**Group J — TIER 9: Phenomenology with Nulls (Phase 8; the program meets data)**

37 - **Run pre-registered nulls in parallel with every prediction** — the rule that makes results evidence.
38 - **The ordered empirical shapes**: colored-noise spectrum (T2); Diophantine angular statistics (T3); interferometry scaling $V(N,m,t)$ (T2, pre-registered exponent); spectral dimension; relic channel.
39 - **The galactic stress test runs last and only after gravitating-Q is derived**, against the Verlinde null model and with the CMB third-peak graveyard gate as the explicit terminal condition. *A confirmed particle detection anywhere = the program's death certificate, pre-accepted.*
40 - **The CMB fine-structure channel** (log-periodic/oscillation family) — highest information, highest self-deception risk: pipelines frozen, trials corrected, rival-with-features null constructed before looking.

**Group K — STANDING LAYERS (continuous, every tier)**

41 - **Ledger discipline**: every borrowed ladder enters with a `CLAIM_ID`, gate mapping, and status tag; no survey prestige converts a [SPECULATIVE-SHAPE] to promoted; silent subtractions prohibited — every removal is logged with its problem refiled as debt.
42 - **Red-team cadence**: external pass at every tier boundary; seduction-audit for any analogy or borrowed result doing load-bearing logical work; unpromotion executed without ceremony when triggered.
43 - **Occam checkpoints (D20–D24)**: scheduled at each tier boundary — is the arithmetic layer still earning its keep? Is AS? Is BM's trajectory ontology? Is E8? Is the protocol itself? The program must remain able to lose to its own components.

**Group L — THE KILL MAP (the decision spine)**

44 - Tier 1 kill: no faithful $F_L$ → arithmetic branch stops. Tier 2 kill: weak mixing, no tame factor (×2 classes) → central gate failed, pivot or terminate. Tier 3 kill: no unique measure or unusable regularity → Born chain dead. Tier 5 kill: scheme-dependent quantities → artifacts, re-scope. Tier 6 kill: no-signaling failure, generator wrong, guidance unrecovered → immediate. Tier 8 kill: E→L failure or Lorentz recovery failure → not a TOE candidate, say so. Any tier: the simpler null model wins on all surviving observables → the guillotine falls and the honest summary is published.

**Group M — PRIORITY LOGIC (why this order)**

45 - **Cost gradient honored**: Tier 0–2 are weeks-scale and can kill the program; Tiers 3–5 are months-scale and can kill the classical-ladder version; Tiers 6–9 are the long campaign — *cheap kills always precede expensive derivations*, and every tier's outputs are inputs to exactly one downstream tier, keeping the dependency graph acyclic.
46 - **Two-path protection**: every tier has an escalate-within-budget branch and a terminate-with-dignity branch; the program never faces a binary of "continue or fudge."
47 - **The unique-burden rule**: Tiers 2–4 (amplitude, measure, bridges) get resourcing priority over Tiers 8–9 because the survey showed these are the gates *no rival has opened or passed* — the landscape confirms the chain shape works and supplies ladders for the rest, but the program's reason to exist lives exactly at Tiers 2–4, and the roadmap is built to find out fast whether that reason is real.

---

**Status note:** the roadmap's design principle is the charter's own, applied to scheduling: every tier is a controlled experiment whose most likely honest outcome is branch elimination, the borrowed ladders (tensor-RG, CDT probes, CFS projections, Einstein–Langevin, the HL failure map, the LQG counting cross-check) are slotted where they supply established mathematics for named gates rather than decoration, and the whole structure terminates in published obituaries rather than defended corpses. The first ninety days are Tier 0 through Tier 2 — and if the Kronecker spectrum comes back empty twice, the program will have spent less than a season to learn what no decade of narrative-building would have taught: that the music needs a different storm.
