# Black Hole Information Paradox Through the BM–IST–AS Lens

Wearing the thought-experiment hat, and setting aside any commitment to whether the tripartite synthesis is true, here is what it suggests about the black hole information paradox.

The paradox, in one sentence: Hawking radiation appears thermal, so the information that fell into the black hole seems lost, violating unitarity. The tripartite synthesis does not yet solve this. But it reframes it in a way that makes three previously separate resolutions into one—and that reframing is tantalizing.

## The reframing

In the tripartite picture:

- The **UV substrate** is a deterministic, discrete, arithmetic dynamical system `(X, F)`. Information is never destroyed here; the dynamics is reversible or at least information-preserving in the substrate's own terms.
- The **IR effective theory** is Bohmian mechanics (or its relativistic extension). This is where black holes, horizons, Hawking radiation, and unitarity appear as effective notions.
- The **RG flow** connects them. Coarse-graining over arithmetic degrees of freedom produces dissipation, noise, and the appearance of information loss.

So the paradox is not about information being destroyed. It is about information being **redistributed into degrees of freedom that the effective theory does not track**.

This is exactly the structure of an open quantum system: a pure state becomes mixed when you trace out an unobserved bath. In the tripartite synthesis, the bath is the arithmetic sector.

## Tantalizing insights

### 1. Hawking radiation is thermal because of the H-theorem, not because information is lost

Valentini's H-theorem says non-equilibrium distributions relax to equilibrium. The black hole is a highly complex, scrambling system. Its coarse-grained state relaxes to the thermal distribution. That is why Hawking radiation looks thermal. But the fine-grained correlations that carry the information are still there—they are encoded in the arithmetic texture of the substrate. The H-theorem explains the thermal appearance; unitarity of the substrate explains the information preservation.

This is the cleanest resolution the tripartite offers: **thermal radiation is the equilibrium description; information is in the deviations from equilibrium**.

### 2. The Page curve emerges from the RG flow of entanglement entropy

As the black hole evaporates, the relevant RG scale changes. The entanglement entropy between the black hole and the radiation is not a fundamental quantity; it is an effective one, computed by coarse-graining over arithmetic modes. The turnover at the Page time might correspond to the scale where the arithmetic bath becomes dominant. In this picture, the Page curve is not put in by hand—it is a consequence of the flow.

This is speculative, but it is a concrete target: if the tripartite program ever constructs a discrete RG flow, the Page curve becomes a computable prediction.

### 3. Islands are effective descriptions of invariant-set correlations

The island formula says that after the Page time, the entanglement wedge of the radiation includes the black hole interior. In the tripartite synthesis, the interior is not a separate region in the UV. It is an effective description of correlations in the invariant set `I_U`. The "island" is where the arithmetic substrate correlates interior and exterior degrees of freedom. This is not nonlocality in the usual sense—it is the global structure of the invariant set showing up in the effective theory.

### 4. The singularity is replaced by a critical surface, not a breakdown of physics

Asymptotic Safety provides a UV fixed point where the continuum metric description breaks down. Invariant Set Theory provides a discrete arithmetic substrate. Bohmian mechanics provides definite trajectories that continue through the crossover. The singularity is not a place where information is lost; it is a scale where the effective description changes. The tripartite suggests that the singularity is a **crossover phenomenon**, not a fundamental obstruction.

### 5. The firewall paradox dissolves

The firewall paradox assumes that local quantum field theory applies across the horizon. The tripartite says that assumption fails at the crossover scale. The horizon is an IR construct; at the UV scale, geometry is fuzzy and arithmetic. There is no sharp horizon where a firewall could form. The paradox is an artifact of forcing a continuum description where it does not apply.

### 6. Bekenstein-Hawking entropy as counting arithmetic states

The tripartite synthesis has a natural candidate for black hole entropy: it is the number of admissible arithmetic states that project to the same coarse-grained geometry. This is a microscopic derivation of `S = A/4G`—not an assumption, but a counting problem. If the program succeeds, this would be a major result.

### 7. Deviations from thermality as a test

If the arithmetic substrate leaves fine structure in the effective theory, then Hawking radiation should not be exactly thermal. There should be small deviations—Diophantine in character—that carry the missing information. These deviations are extremely small for astrophysical black holes, but they might be detectable in analog black hole systems or primordial black holes. This is a tantalizing experimental handle.

## The deepest tantalizing connection

The tripartite synthesis unifies three separate strategies for resolving the information paradox:

- **Hidden variables** (BM): information is in definite configurations that the wavefunction does not track.
- **Discrete substrate** (IST): information is bounded and stored in arithmetic degrees of freedom.
- **UV completion** (AS): information is preserved by the flow and only appears lost in the IR.

Together, they suggest a picture where the black hole information paradox is not a paradox about information loss. It is a signal of the crossover between the IR effective theory and the UV arithmetic substrate—a signal that could potentially be detected as a deviation from exact thermality.

## Honest caveats

This is all speculative. It depends on the tripartite program actually constructing:

- the discrete RG flow,
- the invariant measure,
- the projection from substrate to effective theory,
- and the recovery of relativistic QFT and GR.

None of these exists yet. But the reframing is compelling because it turns the information paradox from a crisis into a **prediction**: if the tripartite synthesis is correct, black hole evaporation should not be exactly thermal. That is a testable claim, and it is the kind of claim the program needs.

In classical General Relativity, the information paradox begins because geodesics terminate at $r=0$, creating a physical "sink" where matter-energy and its information content are excised from spacetime.

* **Bohmian Mechanics Contribution:** The scale-dependent quantum potential functional, geometrized via the Weyl scalar curvature:

* **Synthesis Insight:** The repulsive quantum potential counteracts gravitational collapse before a singularity can form. The collapsing star undergoes a non-singular Planckian bounce or stabilizes into a dense, non-singular remnant core. Because spacetime never terminates, information is never crushed out of existence.

### 2. Ontic Invertibility: Information Loss as a Mathematical Impossibility (BM + IST)

Standard Hawking radiation poses a paradox because a pure quantum state appears to evolve into a mixed, thermal state, violating the unitarity postulate of quantum mechanics.

* **Trajectory Determinism:** In this tripartite framework, the underlying ontology consists of deterministic field trajectories $\phi_k(x)$ guided by the phase $S_k[\phi]$ of the scale-dependent wave-functional $\Psi_k[\phi]$:



$$\frac{\partial\phi_k(x)}{\partial\tau} = \left.\frac{\delta S_k[\phi]}{\delta\phi_k(x)}\right\vert{}_{\phi=\phi_k}$$


* **Synthesis Insight:** Information loss requires phase-space trajectories to merge or terminate. Because the guidance equations on the fractal invariant manifold $I_U$ are deterministic and single-valued, trajectory flow is a bijective map. The exact microscopic state of the collapsing matter is deterministically mapped into the outgoing radiation field trajectories. Unitarity is preserved not as an abstract Hilbert space rotation, but as exact, deterministic trajectory invertibility.

### 3. Hawking "Thermalization" as a P-Adic Coarse-Graining Artifact (IST + AS)

If information is preserved, why does Hawking radiation appear featureless and thermal to an outside observer?

* **Fractal Dimensional Reduction:** At high energy scales $k$, Asymptotic Safety drives spacetime spectral dimension reduction ($d_s \to 2$). IST reflects this by constraining cosmological states to a non-computable fractal set $I_U$ with a finite $P$-adic order parameter $p(k)$.


* **Synthesis Insight:** Continuous complex Hilbert spaces ($\mathcal{H}_3$) are merely an infrared (IR) approximation ($p \to \infty$). Near the horizon/core, where energy scales reach Planckian values, $p(k)$ is finite, and dynamics are governed by the non-Archimedean $P$-adic metric:



$$d_p(x,y) = p^{-k}$$


* Hawking radiation appears thermal only because standard quantum observers project a discrete, $P$-adic fractal trajectory dynamics onto a continuous, linear Hilbert space. The "loss of information" and apparent thermal entropy are mathematical illusions caused by coarse-graining the fractal structure of $I_U$ into continuous spacetime modes. The subtle correlations are not lost; they are encoded in the $P$-adic metric distances of the state space.

The Almheiri-Marolf-Polchinski-Sully (AMPS) firewall paradox states that if outgoing Hawking quanta are entangled with early radiation to preserve information, they cannot simultaneously be entangled with their infalling partners across the horizon without violating the Monogamy of Entanglement, implying a high-energy "firewall" at the horizon.

* **Non-Computable State Constraints:** Invariant Set Theory evades Bell-type no-go theorems and monogamy conflicts by violating the Measurement Independence assumption. Counterfactual states (alternative measurement choices or alternate particle pair states) that do not belong to the fractal set $I_U$ are physically undefined.


* **Synthesis Insight:** The firewall paradox relies on superposing hypothetical, counterfactual entangled states across a continuous horizon. In the BM+IST+AS framework, states off the invariant manifold $I_U$ do not exist. Because the path-integral states required to generate a firewall correspond to trajectories off $I_U$, the firewall configuration is mathematically unrepresentable within the theory. An infalling observer crosses the horizon region smoothly without encountering an artificial boundary of infinite energy.

### 5. Scale-Dependent Horizon Traversal and Information Egress (AS + BM)

Classical event horizons act as one-way causal membranes due to fixed light-cone structures.

* **Dynamized Local Scale Parameter:** By replacing a global cutoff scale $k$ with a local, field-dependent scale parameter $k(x)$ governed by the local Bohmian quantum potential, the effective spacetime metric $g_{\mu\nu}(k(x))$ becomes dynamically responsive to local quantum gradients.


* **Synthesis Insight:** Near the region where a classical horizon would form, the local energy scale $k(x)$ spikes, shifting the running metric $g_{\mu\nu}(k)$ away from classical Schwarzschild behavior. The velocity field of outgoing field modes:



$$v_k^\mu(x) = \frac{1}{m} g_k^{\mu\nu}(x) \partial_\nu S_k(x)$$



gains contributions from the scale-dependent phase gradient $\partial_\nu S_k$. The metric modification opens "scale-dependent channels," allowing Bohmian trajectories to gradually guide energy and encoded information back out into asymptotically flat space, dissolving the strict causal trapping of the classical event horizon.

## claude

Dropping the ledger hat for this one — here's where the pieces actually rhyme, with the important caveat stated once up front rather than after every sentence: **most of the individual ingredients below already exist in the literature** (asymptotically-safe black holes, ER=EPR, the islands/replica-wormhole story, horizon complementarity). What's genuinely new is what happens when you run them through a framework that has BM's hidden-variable ontology, IST's literal finite-information substrate, and AS's RG machinery *all three, simultaneously, forced to talk to each other*. A few places, that combination says something none of the three says alone.

### 1. Information isn't destroyed at the horizon — it was never going to be destroyed anywhere, and the black hole is just the sharpest instance of a mechanism this synthesis already needed for completely unrelated reasons

Go all the way back to the very first substantive insight explored in this whole thread: decoherence, reframed not as information loss but as information *redistribution* — bits leaving the visible sector don't vanish, they move into a larger entangled register (the p-adic bath, in IST language; the traced-out environment, in ordinary decoherence language). At the time, that idea was flagged as too weak to derive individual trajectories on its own. But notice what it was never weak at: it's a *general conservation law* for the substrate, derived from nothing black-hole-specific. Apply it to a horizon and you get the modern resolution (information comes out correlated in the Hawking radiation) for free, as a special case of a mechanism the architecture already had to build for decoherence in an ordinary lab. The tripartite synthesis doesn't need a new principle for the black hole — it needs the black hole to stop being treated as a special case at all. That's a real conceptual economy, and it's very much in the spirit of §31's rule 2: retain successful mathematics, not inherited metaphysics — here, "the horizon is special" turns out to be the inherited metaphysics.

### 2. Finite \(L\) doesn't need replica wormholes to get the Page curve — it needed them less than semiclassical gravity did

The Page curve — entanglement entropy of the radiation rising, then turning over as the black hole finishes evaporating — was a genuine puzzle for semiclassical gravity because nothing in the effective field theory says the total Hilbert space is finite-dimensional. The fix (Penington; Almheiri, Engelhardt, Marolf, Maxfield) was to find *extra saddle points* in the gravitational path integral (replica wormholes, entanglement islands) that smuggle finiteness back in from the gravitational side. IST's \(L\) is a much blunter instrument aimed at the same target: if the substrate underlying a bounded region only ever had \(L\) bits of capacity, the Page-curve turnover isn't something you have to go looking for in extra topologies — it's baked into the microscopic state space from the start. The tantalizing (and appropriately hedged) reading: replica wormholes might be effective field theory's roundabout way of *rediscovering*, saddle-point by saddle-point, a finiteness the substrate has natively. If `IST-L-CFUNC-1` (\(L\) tied to an RG-flow \(c\)-function) ever gets built, there'd be a genuinely sharp question sitting right there: does the microscopically-derived \(L\)-bound reproduce the *same* Page curve the island formula gives, or a different one? That's a real, checkable convergence test between two approaches that currently have no contact with each other.

### 3. The firewall argument leans on an assumption BM doesn't have to make
