Here are 10 names that fit the POC’s architecture and feel like a real systems project rather than a mythology-themed demo:

| #      | Name              | Why it fits                                                                                                                       |
| ------ | ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**  | **Aegis**         | Protection, authority, and controlled passage. Strong fit for a trust/verification substrate.                                     |
| **2**  | **Styx**          | The boundary that must be crossed under binding conditions; excellent metaphor for consequential authorization.                   |
| **3**  | **Gate of Hades** | Dramatic version of the passage metaphor: once crossed, consequences matter. Better for an internal codename than a product name. |
| **4**  | **Delphi**        | The Oracle association makes it immediately intelligible; strong fit for epistemic state and consultation.                        |
| **5**  | **Argus**         | The many-eyed watcher; excellent metaphor for provenance, evidence, and continuous scrutiny.                                      |
| **6**  | **Janus**         | The two-faced guardian of thresholds; particularly fitting for the boundary between epistemic state and external action.          |
| **7**  | **Nemesis**       | Consequence and retribution when something violates the governing order; good for adversarial verification.                       |
| **8**  | **Cerberus**      | Three-headed guardian at the boundary; nicely maps to multiple gates such as evidence, authority, and action.                     |
| **9**  | **Moirai**        | The Fates govern what may proceed and what cannot be undone; evocative for constrained state transitions.                         |
| **10** | **Labyrinth**     | A system where claims, evidence, dependencies, and consequences must be navigated without losing provenance.                      |

### My top three

**Aegis** — best overall project name.
**Argus** — best name if you want the emphasis on verification and evidence.
**Styx** — best name if you want the emphasis on consequential boundaries and authorization.

For the architecture itself, I particularly like:

> **ARGUS** — the Trust Verification POC
> **Oracle** — epistemic query
> **Sphinx** — authorization gate
> **Styx** — consequential boundary
> **Ferryman** — execution/effect
> **Chronicler** — history

That gives the system a coherent mythology rather than merely naming individual screens.
